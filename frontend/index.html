Ratna’s Modern Computer Training Institute

Real skills grow with real practice.

Founded in 2019, RMCTI provides quality computer training in a close-knit, supportive environment. Every student gets the personal attention they need to build confidence and succeed.

With modern computers and experienced faculty, we focus on practical, hands-on learning that prepares students for real-world digital tasks.

About RMCTI

Est. 2019

Focused Digital Education

Founded in 2019, Ratna's Modern Computer Training Institute (RMCTI) has been dedicated to providing quality computer training in a close-knit, supportive environment. Our mission is to ensure every student gets the personal attention they need to succeed.

With modern computers and experienced faculty, we focus on practical, hands-on learning that prepares our students for real-world digital tasks in a friendly tuition setting.
